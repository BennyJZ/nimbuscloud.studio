6. Customs, duties and import VAT

For deliveries outside the shipping origin's customs area, local import duties or VAT may apply on arrival. These charges are set by your country's customs authority and are your responsibility — they are not part of the price you pay us, and we cannot calculate or refund them.

If a parcel is returned to us because an import charge was refused, we refund the goods minus the delivery and any return-carriage cost.

7. Wrong or incomplete addresses

Please check your delivery address at checkout. If a parcel is returned because the address was wrong or incomplete, we will resend it once you cover the new delivery cost.

If you spot a mistake, email us immediately — we can usually correct it before dispatch.

8. Late, lost or damaged parcels

Late. If your order has not arrived within the expected window, contact us and we will chase the carrier.

Lost. Carriers normally require a waiting period before declaring a parcel lost. Once that passes, you get a replacement or a full refund — your choice.

Damaged. Tell us as soon as you notice, with a photo of the item and packaging if you can. We cover return postage and replace or refund in full. Photos help us claim against the carrier, but we will not refuse a claim for lack of them.

Until the goods are delivered to you, the risk stays with us. A parcel lost in transit is our problem to resolve, not yours.

9. Right to cancel

Delivery is separate from your right to change your mind. See our Returns and Refunds page for the 14-day statutory right of withdrawal.

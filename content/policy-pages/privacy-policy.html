2. What we collect and why

When you place an order

We collect your name, email address, billing address, delivery address, phone number (if given) and order details.

  • Purpose: to take payment, deliver your order, handle returns, and keep the accounting records we are legally required to keep.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) for the order itself; legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for accounting and tax records.

We never see or store your full card number. Card details are entered directly with our payment processor.

When you create an account

We store your email address, a hashed password, and your saved addresses and order history.

  • Purpose: so you can log in, see past orders, and check out faster.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

When you just browse

Our hosting provider records technical data including your IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps, in server logs.

  • Purpose: security, fraud prevention, and keeping the site working.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in operating a secure website.

When you contact us

We keep your message and contact details so we can reply and follow up.

  • Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), or performance of a contract if your message concerns an order.

6. How long we keep it

  • Order and accounting records: five years after the end of the financial year, as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act (bokføringsloven § 13).
  • Account data: until you ask us to delete the account.
  • Enquiries and correspondence: up to two years after the matter is closed.
  • Server logs: as retained by our hosting provider, normally a matter of weeks.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15).
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16).
  • Erasure — have your data deleted, where we are not legally required to keep it (Art. 17).
  • Restriction — limit how we use your data while a dispute is resolved (Art. 18).
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller (Art. 20).
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing relies on consent (Art. 7(3)). Withdrawing does not affect processing already carried out.

To use any of these, email the address in section 1. We will respond within one month. We may need to verify your identity first, so that we do not disclose your data to someone else.

8. Complaints

If you think we have handled your data wrongly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority:

  • Datatilsynet, Postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo, Norway — datatilsynet.no

If you live elsewhere in the EEA, you may instead complain to your local supervisory authority.

9. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This store uses:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — WooCommerce uses these to remember your cart and keep you logged in. The store cannot function without them, so they are set without consent, as permitted for strictly necessary cookies.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices such as recently viewed items.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If that changes, we will ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set, and update this policy.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break the cart and checkout.

10. Children

This store is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
